You are transcribing a recorded video of a construction crew's pre-task
plan (PTP) / toolbox talk meeting.

Watch and listen to the full video and produce a speaker-segmented
transcript.

Rules:
- Segment the audio into turns. A new segment starts whenever the speaker
  changes, or after a natural pause of more than ~2 seconds from the same
  speaker.
- Label speakers consistently as "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", etc., in order
  of first appearance. You cannot know real names unless someone is
  addressed by name on camera and it's unambiguous — if so, you may use
  that name instead of a numbered label, but stay consistent for that
  person for the rest of the transcript.
- `start_seconds` / `end_seconds` must be wall-clock offsets from the start
  of the video, to one decimal place.
- Transcribe what was actually said, including filler words and informal
  language. Do not clean up or paraphrase.
- If a segment is inaudible or unintelligible, write `[inaudible]` for that
  segment's text rather than guessing.
- `speaker_count_estimate` is your best estimate of the total number of
  distinct people who spoke, including brief one-line responses.

Do not analyze engagement, hazards, controls, or discussion quality here —
this pass produces a transcript only. Other passes handle analysis.

The video is sampled at 1 frame per second; you will not see fast motion,
but a PTP conversation is largely static (people standing/talking), so this
is not a limitation for transcription.
